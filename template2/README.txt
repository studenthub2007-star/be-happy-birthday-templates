# Template 2 — 20 Second Heart Transition

## Folder structure

template2_heart_transition/
├── index.html
├── style.css
├── script.js
└── assets/
    └── music.mp3   <-- put your own MP3 here

## Run

1. Open the folder in VS Code.
2. Put your music file at `assets/music.mp3`.
3. Open `index.html` with Live Server.
4. The heart intro runs for 20 seconds.
5. NEXT becomes available after 20 seconds.
6. Clicking NEXT opens the full editable birthday page.
7. Click the photo boxes to upload your own photos.

## Change text

Open `index.html` and edit:
- Happy Birthday, Buggiii ❤️
- You are my favourite chapter...
- The long message
- Journey chapter text

This is the first complete base version. More effects such as a 3D photo carousel,
confetti, falling rose petals, custom editor panel, and share/download can be added
on top of this structure.
